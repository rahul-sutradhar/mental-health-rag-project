Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Serenity Mindspace.

What is Serenity Mindspace? ▼
Serenity Mindspace is a mental wellness platform that connects people with compassionate AI support and licensed mental health specialists. You can chat with our AI 24/7 or book text/video sessions with verified professionals.
Is Serenity Mindspace a substitute for emergency care? ▼
No. If you are in immediate danger or experiencing a mental health emergency, please call iCall (9152987821) or Vandrevala Foundation (1860-2662-345) or emergency services (112) immediately. Serenity Mindspace is a support tool, not an emergency service.
Who can use Serenity Mindspace? ▼
Anyone aged 18 or older can create an account. We support users across India and internationally. Specialists must hold valid licences in their practising jurisdiction.
How do I get started? ▼
Sign up for free, complete the optional wellness assessment, and choose your support type — AI chat (first 10 messages free), or a specialist session. You receive 100 welcome tokens on signup.
What are tokens and how do I get them? ▼
Tokens are Serenity Mindspace's virtual currency. You receive 100 free tokens on signup. Additional tokens can be purchased in the Token Store (100 tokens for ₹99, 500 for ₹449, 1,000 for ₹799). You can also win 50 bonus tokens daily via the Lucky Vault (1% chance).
How many tokens does each service cost? ▼
AI chat: first 10 messages are free, then 5 tokens per message. Specialist text chat: 50 tokens per 30-minute session. Specialist video call: 100 tokens per 30-minute session.
Are tokens refundable? ▼
Tokens are generally non-refundable once used. If a session could not be completed due to a verifiable platform error or specialist no-show, please contact support within 48 hours for a review.
Do tokens expire? ▼
Tokens do not expire as long as your account remains active. Accounts inactive for more than 12 months may be subject to token forfeiture with 30 days' notice.
How are specialists verified? ▼
Every specialist submits their government ID, degree certificate, and practising licence. Our admin team manually reviews and verifies each document before the specialist's profile goes live. Verified specialists display a blue ✓ badge.
Can I choose my specialist? ▼
Yes. Browse our Specialists directory, filter by specialisation, language, experience, and availability, and book directly with the specialist of your choice.
What if I'm not happy with a session? ▼
You can rate and review every session. If you have a serious concern about a specialist's conduct, contact support and we will investigate within 48 hours. Documented misconduct results in immediate removal.
How do I become a specialist on Serenity? ▼
Create an account and select "I'm a specialist." Upload your credentials (Aadhaar, degree, practising licence), write your bio, and set your availability. Our admin team will review your application within 3–5 business days.
Are my sessions private? ▼
Yes. Conversations are end-to-end encrypted and only accessible to you and your specialist. We never share your conversation content with third parties for advertising. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
Can I delete my data? ▼
Yes. You can delete your account and all associated data from your profile settings. You may also request data export or specific deletions by emailing privacy@serenitymindspace.com. We respond within 30 days.
Does Serenity sell my data? ▼
Never. We do not sell personal data to advertisers or data brokers. Serenity Mindspace products are ad-free. Data is used only to provide and improve the service as described in our Privacy Policy.
What devices and browsers are supported? ▼
Serenity Mindspace works on all modern browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) on desktop and mobile. Video calls require camera and microphone access. We recommend Chrome or Firefox for the best experience.
The video call isn't working. What should I do? ▼
1. Ensure you've allowed camera and microphone permissions in your browser. 2. Try refreshing the page. 3. Check your internet connection. 4. Try a different browser. 5. If the issue persists, contact support — we'll investigate within 4 hours.
Is there a mobile app? ▼
Currently Serenity Mindspace is a Progressive Web App (PWA) — you can "Add to Home Screen" from your mobile browser for an app-like experience. Dedicated iOS and Android apps are on our roadmap.

Still have questions?

Our support team typically responds within 24 hours.
